Platform admins can track work orders privately under Admin → Work orders: who is contracted, on which Projects (any kind, one line per Project and token with its own amount), over what period, and with what status. Paid so far is calculated from the Agency's approved payouts to any of the contributor's accounts, from the start date until the work order is completed or terminated, so late payments still count. Work orders warn when a line is paid beyond its amount, when active work orders exceed a Project's budget, when one ends within 14 days, and when the contributor has no services agreement on file. The page also lists recent approved payouts that no work order covers. Only drafts can be deleted, and a contributor can have one active work order per Project.
